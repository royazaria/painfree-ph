Is this approved by the Philippine FDA (FDA-PH)?
The PainFree PEMF system holds FDA 510(k) clearance (United States), CE Mark (European Union), and approval from the Israeli Ministry of Health — three of the most rigorous medical device regulatory frameworks in the world. Philippine FDA registration is currently in process. In the meantime, the device is legally importable for use in licensed medical clinics under existing Philippine medical device regulations. We are transparent about where registration stands and will keep partners updated.
What exactly am I buying — a machine, a franchise, or a license?
You are purchasing the medical device itself, along with a clinic partnership agreement that includes: complete clinical protocols, staff training, patient acquisition support, marketing materials, and ongoing technical support. You operate the equipment in your own clinic under your own business. This is not a franchise in the traditional sense — there are no ongoing royalty fees. You own the machine and the revenue it generates.
What is the minimum investment?
The total investment includes the device, shipping, installation, staff training, and initial marketing materials. Investment ranges start from approximately ₱1.5M depending on configuration. We do not publish a fixed price publicly because the right configuration depends on your clinic's specific needs, volume, and setup. The full investment brief — which you can request through our form — includes a complete line-item breakdown.
What happens after I fill out the inquiry form?
Three things, in order: (1) You receive a confirmation within 24 hours — a real person, not a bot. (2) We schedule a 30-minute introductory call to understand your clinic and answer your specific questions. (3) We send you the full investment brief: device specs, ROI model built around your actual numbers, setup timeline, support structure, and financing options. No commitment is required at any stage.
How long until the machine pays for itself?
At 8 sessions/day at ₱2,500/session (conservative), monthly revenue is approximately ₱520,000. At that rate, a ₱1.5M investment has a payback period of roughly 3 months. Use the ROI calculator on our homepage to model your own assumptions. Even conservative scenarios (5 sessions/day at ₱1,800) show payback within 5–6 months.
Is there an ongoing royalty or fee after I buy the machine?
No ongoing royalty fees. You own the machine outright. Revenue from every session is yours. We offer optional ongoing support packages for marketing and clinical protocol updates, but these are not mandatory and not a condition of the partnership.
Do I need a doctor or medical staff to operate it?
PEMF sessions are non-invasive and do not require operator supervision during treatment. A trained clinic attendant can manage sessions once the device is set up. However, we recommend that a licensed physiotherapist or physician conduct the initial patient assessment and treatment protocol design. Our training covers all of this.
Has this been used in the Philippines before?
PainFree PEMF has been operating in 70+ clinics in Israel for 25 years — serving a population of 9 million. We are now bringing this proven system to the Philippines for the first time. We understand that being first-to-market means we need to earn trust with evidence, not just claims — which is why we share published clinical data, medical authority letters, and a transparent investment structure. The first Philippine clinic partners will have our full operational support.
What if the device breaks or needs servicing?
The PainFree PEMF system has an exceptional reliability record across 25+ years of clinic use. In the event of a technical issue: remote diagnostics are available immediately, on-site service is arranged within a defined SLA, and replacement parts are maintained in stock. Full warranty terms are included in the investment brief.
Can I see the device operating before I commit?
Yes. We can arrange a remote demonstration (video call with live device operation) or, for serious investors, a site visit to an operating clinic. This is the right thing to do for a decision of this size. Request a demo through the inquiry form or WhatsApp us directly at +63 962-181-1360.
Will I be competing with other PEMF clinics in my area?
We work with a limited number of partners per city. We discuss your specific location and market during the introductory call and are transparent about whether other clinics are operating or planned nearby. We are not interested in saturating any market — our clinic partners' success is our success.
Why is this 25-year-old Israeli technology only reaching the Philippines now?
The technology is not new — PEMF has been in clinical use worldwide since the 1970s, and PainFree's specific system has operated in Israel since 1999. The Philippines expansion is deliberate and timed to the growing private healthcare sector. Direct answer: we are expanding now because private clinic infrastructure, patient willingness to pay cash for non-surgical pain treatment, and the regulatory environment are all at an inflection point. First-mover advantage in your city is real.
